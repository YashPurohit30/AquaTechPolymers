Adhesive developed for bonding applications involved in the manufacturing of crepe paper tubes used in the transformer and electrical insulation industry.

The adhesive is designed to provide effective and consistent bonding between paper layers, supporting the production of durable and dimensionally stable crepe paper tubes.

## Built for your production line

Whether you manufacture crepe paper tubes for transformer insulation or other electrical applications, our team can work with you to identify the adhesive formulation best suited to your production process.
