The PBA series covers Aqua Tech Polymers' bonding adhesives for distribution transformer manufacturing, available in three grades — PBA 90R, PBA 93 MV and PBA 96MHV — selected according to your substrate, application method and production process.

## Built around your process

Every manufacturing line is different. Material combinations, application methods, production speeds and curing conditions vary from one distribution transformer manufacturer to another, so PBA grades are matched — and where needed customised — to fit yours, rather than asking you to adapt to a fixed formulation.
