Distribution transformer production is volume work where batch-to-batch consistency matters. A compound that behaves differently from one lot to the next forces the line to re-tune.

## What we supply

PBA series bonding adhesive (PBA 90R, PBA 93 MV, PBA 96MHV) for core-to-coil and structural bonding, and CPT crepe paper tube adhesive for insulation components — both available with customised characteristics based on your process.
