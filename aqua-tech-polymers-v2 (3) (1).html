What we solve

Problems that start in the coil shop, end on the field.

01

Windings that loosen and hum

Under thermal cycling, poorly bonded coils shift and vibrate. Our high-build impregnation gums fill the interstices and lock the winding as a solid mass, cutting audible noise and mechanical fatigue.

02

Insulation that fails early

Moisture ingress and abrasion degrade dielectric performance long before the rated life. Our Class F and Class H varnish systems seal windings and cores against both, holding dielectric strength through the service life.

03

Batches that don't behave alike

A compound that cures differently lot to lot forces your line to re-tune constantly. We control raw materials and cure profiles to a fixed specification, and retain samples so any batch can be traced back.

04

Long waits for qualification

Switching suppliers usually means months of trials. We despatch samples in about 72 hours and support your qualification with data sheets, COAs and on-call technical help.

Where it goes

Equipment our compounds are built into

Open any application to see which series applies.

Distribution transformers

+

Volume production where consistency matters more than anything. Core-plate varnish on laminations, ATP-B bonding for the core-to-coil assembly, and ATP-R impregnation to lock the winding and cut hum. Supplied in bulk packs sized for continuous line feeding.

Power transformers

+

High-voltage units where thermal endurance is non-negotiable. Class H systems throughout — ATP-R22 impregnating resin and ATP-S sealing compounds on tank and bushing joints, both selected for long-term stability under load and transformer oil exposure.

Motors and generators

+

Rotating machines put windings under continuous mechanical stress. ATP-R impregnation via VPI or trickle process rigidises the winding against centrifugal load, while ATP-V varnish protects the exposed surfaces from abrasion and moisture.

Current transformers and sensors

+

Small encapsulated assemblies where the compound is the housing. ATP-E60 epoxy potting gives electrical isolation, mechanical protection and moisture resistance in a single pour, with low-exotherm grades for thicker sections.

Reactors, chokes and switchgear

+

Vibration damping and insulation in one. ATP-B bonding locks laminated cores against buzz, ATP-V varnish insulates panel and busbar assemblies, and ATP-S seals enclosures against dust and moisture ingress.

Common questions

Before you enquire

Can I get a sample before committing?

+

Yes. Trial quantities go out in roughly 72 hours with the technical data sheet and safety data sheet, so you can run the material on your own line under your own conditions before making any commitment.

What if no stock grade fits my process?

+

We formulate in-house, so we can adjust viscosity, gel time, cure schedule or thermal class to suit your existing oven capacity and line speed. Tell us the constraint and we'll work within it.

Do you supply documentation for audits?

+

Every lot ships with a certificate of analysis. Technical and safety data sheets are available for all grades, and we hold retained samples so any batch can be re-tested and traced if a question comes up later.

What are the minimum order quantities?

+

Minimums vary by grade and packing format. Trial quantities are much smaller than production packs — share your expected monthly consumption and we'll quote the packing and quantity that makes sense.

Do you export outside India?

+

Yes, with export packing and documentation. Shipping and lead times depend on destination and the chemistry being shipped, since some grades carry transport classifications — we'll confirm both when quoting.

Send us your spec. We'll send back a grade.

Thermal class, cure method, substrate and volume — that's all we need to shortlist something and get a sample moving.
